## Generated applications

`pliego new` does not automatically license an application's new original code,
content, brand, or assets. Generated projects contain `LICENSE.md` as an
explicit placeholder. Their authors must choose and install an application
license before distribution. Unmodified template code remains GPL-covered; the
placeholder is not an exception or relicensing grant for copied PliegoRS
template code. This preserves ownership of genuinely original user work, but it
does not waive obligations that may arise when an application is distributed as
a derivative or combined work with GPL-covered PliegoRS software.

## Historical releases

Relicensing is prospective. Artifacts and source revisions already distributed
under Apache-2.0, including `v0.4.0-beta.1` and earlier releases, remain usable
under the irrevocable terms previously granted. The GPL and CC licenses govern
the new source revisions carrying their SPDX identifiers; they do not withdraw
prior Apache-2.0 permissions.

stored beside those assets. Trademarks are not licensed under GPL or Creative
